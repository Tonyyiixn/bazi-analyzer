# Da Yun / Luck Pillars (大運)

The natal chart (Year/Month/Day/Hour pillars) is fixed at birth and describes
a person's baseline nature. Da Yun are ten-year "luck pillars" that overlay
shifting stem/branch energy on top of that fixed chart, and are the primary
tool for timing *when* something in the chart tends to activate.

## How to read a Da Yun period
1. Treat the Da Yun's stem and branch as a temporary 5th pillar added to the
   natal chart for that ~10-year window.
2. Determine its Ten God relationship to the Day Master, same as any other
   stem (see ten_gods_overview).
3. Check whether it reinforces or drains the Day Master (does it add to a
   strength the chart needs, or pile onto something already excessive?).
4. Check for clashes/combinations against the natal branches, especially the
   Day branch and Month branch (see branch_clashes_and_combinations).

## General timing heuristics
- A Da Yun that brings a Ten God the Day Master is currently lacking (e.g.
  Resource support arriving for a weak Day Master) tends to read as a
  favorable, stabilizing decade.
- A Da Yun that piles more of an already-excessive Ten God onto the chart
  (e.g. more Wealth/Officer pressure onto an already-weak Day Master) tends
  to read as a strained or overwhelming decade.
- A Da Yun stem or branch that clashes the natal Day branch is traditionally
  treated with more caution - often linked to upheaval in foundational life
  areas (home, health, core relationships) during that window, not
  necessarily "bad" but disruptive/transitional.

## Practical reading note
Da Yun analysis should always be relative to the natal chart's specific
needs (per Day Master strength), never read in isolation. The same Da Yun
stem can be excellent for one person and destabilizing for another depending
entirely on what their base chart already has too much or too little of.
