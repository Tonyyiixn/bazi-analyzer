# Ten Gods Overview (十神)

The Ten Gods describe the relationship between another stem and the Day
Master, based on two factors: the generating/controlling cycle between their
elements, and whether their yin/yang polarity matches the Day Master's.

| Relationship to Day Master | Same polarity | Opposite polarity |
|---|---|---|
| Same element as Day Master | Friend (比肩) | Rob Wealth (劫財) |
| Generates the Day Master | Indirect Resource (偏印) | Direct Resource (正印) |
| Generated by the Day Master | Hurting Officer (傷官) | Eating God (食神) |
| Controlled by the Day Master | Indirect Wealth (偏財) | Direct Wealth (正財) |
| Controls the Day Master | Seven Killings (七殺) | Direct Officer (正官) |

## Rough thematic groupings
- **Resource stars (印)**: learning, credentials, mentorship, being supported/
  nurtured. Direct Resource = formal/institutional support; Indirect Resource
  = unconventional/self-taught support.
- **Friend/Rob Wealth (比劫)**: peers, independence, competition, willpower.
  Rob Wealth in particular is associated with competing for resources/money.
- **Output stars (食傷)**: self-expression, creativity, talent, what you
  produce. Eating God = refined, disciplined output; Hurting Officer = raw,
  rebellious, rule-breaking output.
- **Wealth stars (財)**: money, tangible reward, what you pursue/control.
  Direct Wealth = steady, earned income; Indirect Wealth = windfalls,
  business risk, speculative gain.
- **Officer/Authority stars (官殺)**: structure, discipline, external
  authority (bosses, law, institutions), and for many traditional charts,
  the spouse-star for women. Direct Officer = legitimate authority/rules;
  Seven Killings = harsh pressure/competition, or self-discipline if tamed.

This table is a starting map, not a substitute for looking at *where* each
star sits (which pillar) and how strong the Day Master is - the same star can
read very differently in a strong vs. weak chart.
