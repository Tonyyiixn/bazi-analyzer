# Day Master Strength (身強身弱)

The Day Master (日主) is the Heavenly Stem of the Day Pillar - it represents the
self, and its relative strength or weakness is the single most load-bearing
judgment in a Bazi reading, since it determines which Ten Gods are "helpful" vs
"harmful" for the chart.

## What strengthens the Day Master
- Being born in its own season (e.g. a Wood Day Master born in spring).
- Resource stars (印) in the stems - the element that generates the Day Master.
- Rob Wealth / Friend stars (比劫) - other stems/branches of the same element as
  the Day Master.
- Root support: a matching or generating element hidden in the branches,
  especially the Day branch itself.

## What weakens the Day Master
- Being born in a season that controls or drains it (e.g. a Wood Day Master
  born in autumn, a Metal season that controls Wood).
- Heavy Output stars (食傷 - Eating God / Hurting Officer) draining energy out.
- Heavy Wealth stars (財) - the element the Day Master controls, which taxes it.
- Heavy Officer/Authority stars (官殺) - the element that controls the Day
  Master, when unsupported by Resource.

## Why it matters for a reading
A strong Day Master generally wants more Output, Wealth, and Officer influence
(it has the reserves to spend). A weak Day Master generally wants more
Resource and Rob Wealth support (it needs reinforcement before it can afford
to "spend" through Output/Wealth/Officer). Getting this backwards is the most
common beginner mistake in interpretation - e.g. telling a weak-Day-Master
person to chase an aggressive Wealth-heavy career when their chart shows them
already overwhelmed by Wealth/Officer pressure.
