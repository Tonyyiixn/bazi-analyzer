# Resource Stars and Friend/Rob Wealth Stars (印星 / 比劫)

These two groups both *support* the Day Master rather than task it - Resource
generates the Day Master, Friend/Rob Wealth shares the Day Master's own
element. They're generally what a weak Day Master needs more of.

## Direct Resource (正印) vs. Indirect Resource (偏印)
- **Direct Resource**: formal, institutional support - traditional education,
  credentials, a nurturing parent figure, legitimate qualifications. Linked
  to conventional learning and being taken care of through recognized
  channels.
- **Indirect Resource**: unconventional support - self-taught skill, niche
  expertise, unusual mentorship, spiritual/philosophical inclination.
  Sometimes read as more independent-minded learning, or (in heavier
  traditional readings) a more detached/unconventional relationship to
  nurturing figures.

## Friend (比肩) vs. Rob Wealth (劫財)
- **Friend**: peers, siblings, collaborators, straightforward same-element
  support. Associated with cooperation, willpower, and steady self-reliance.
- **Rob Wealth**: same energy but opposite polarity - associated with
  competition, and traditionally with "sharing" or losing wealth/resources to
  rivals or unexpected expenses (the name literally means "robs wealth").
  Not purely negative - it also represents assertiveness, risk tolerance, and
  the ability to fight for what you want.

## Why these matter for career/life direction readings
A chart heavy in Resource stars often points toward learning-driven paths
(research, academia, credentialed expertise) before the person is ready to
"spend" that knowledge via Output/Wealth/Officer. A chart heavy in Friend/Rob
Wealth suggests someone who does well building things through peer networks,
partnerships, or competitive environments rather than working alone under a
single authority figure.
