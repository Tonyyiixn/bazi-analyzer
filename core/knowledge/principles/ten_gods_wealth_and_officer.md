# Wealth Stars and Officer/Authority Stars (財星 / 官殺)

Both Wealth and Officer are elements the Day Master must "engage with" rather
than simply possess - Wealth is controlled BY the Day Master, Officer/Killings
CONTROLS the Day Master. Both are only genuinely beneficial when the Day
Master is strong enough to handle them; on a weak Day Master they tend to read
as burden rather than benefit.

## Direct Wealth (正財) vs. Indirect Wealth (偏財)
- **Direct Wealth**: steady, earned income - a salary, a stable business,
  wealth built through consistent effort. Also traditionally linked to a
  disciplined, practical relationship to money and, for men, to the wife-star
  in some traditional readings.
- **Indirect Wealth**: windfalls, speculative gains, business risk-taking,
  wealth that comes in larger irregular amounts. Associated with
  entrepreneurial or opportunistic money-making, and a more generous/
  easy-come-easy-go relationship to money.

## Direct Officer (正官) vs. Seven Killings (七殺)
- **Direct Officer**: legitimate authority, rules, promotion through proper
  channels, reputation, discipline that comes from external structure (a job
  title, the law, social expectation). Read as generally protective when
  balanced - it gives the Day Master direction.
- **Seven Killings**: raw pressure, competition, danger, or forceful
  authority. When unchecked (no Eating God/Resource to tame it) it can read
  as harsh circumstances or conflict. When controlled - e.g. by a strong
  Eating God, or supported by Resource - Seven Killings is traditionally
  prized as a sign of someone who can handle extreme pressure and emerge
  with real authority (a "tamed" Seven Killings chart).

## Reading rule of thumb
Check Day Master strength FIRST. A strong Day Master with prominent
Wealth/Officer generally reads as ambition realized (money and status the
person can actually hold onto). A weak Day Master with the same stars
prominent often reads as being "used up" by obligations - working hard for
money or status that drains rather than rewards, until Resource/Rob Wealth
support strengthens the Day Master (often via a favorable Da Yun).
