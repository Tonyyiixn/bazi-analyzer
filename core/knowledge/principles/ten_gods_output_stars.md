# Output Stars: Eating God vs. Hurting Officer (食神 / 傷官)

Both are generated by the Day Master (the element the Day Master produces),
representing self-expression and output - but their opposite-vs-same polarity
to the Day Master gives them very different temperaments.

## Eating God (食神) - opposite polarity to the Day Master
Traditionally read as the gentler, more refined Output star. Associated with:
- Enjoyment of craft, food, art, leisure, and disciplined creative output.
- A calmer temperament - expresses itself without needing to confront
  authority directly.
- When strong and unclashed, often linked to good health, contentment, and a
  talent that can be monetized steadily (it also has a controlling
  relationship to Seven Killings, traditionally read as "Eating God controls
  Seven Killings" - i.e. disciplined talent can neutralize harsh pressure).

## Hurting Officer (傷官) - opposite polarity
The more rebellious, unconventional Output star. Associated with:
- Original thinking, individualism, dislike of rigid rules or being told
  what to do.
- Strong critical/verbal ability - can be eloquent or cutting.
- Career fit: creative fields, entrepreneurship, performance, anything that
  rewards standing out rather than fitting in.
- Traditional caution: Hurting Officer is said to clash conceptually with
  Direct Officer (rule-breaking vs. legitimate authority) - a chart with both
  prominent can suggest internal tension between wanting to innovate and
  needing to follow structure, or friction with bosses/institutions.

## Practical reading note
Don't treat "Hurting Officer = bad" or "Eating God = good" - both are Output
stars and both can be excellent depending on the rest of the chart. The
distinction is *style* of self-expression (refined/steady vs. rebellious/
sharp), not quality.
