# Five Elements: Generating and Controlling Cycles (五行相生相剋)

The Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) relate to each other
through two cycles. Almost every relationship in a Bazi chart - Ten Gods,
Da Yun interactions, yearly forecasts - reduces to one of these two cycles
applied to a specific pair of elements.

## Generating cycle (相生) - each element produces the next
Wood generates Fire (fuel) -> Fire generates Earth (ash) -> Earth generates
Metal (ore) -> Metal generates Water (condensation) -> Water generates Wood
(growth) -> back to Wood.

Being generated is usually supportive/nourishing. Generating something else,
however, can be *draining* to the element doing the generating - this is the
basis of Output stars (食傷) draining the Day Master.

## Controlling cycle (相剋) - each element restrains another
Wood controls Earth (roots break soil) -> Earth controls Water (dams/absorbs)
-> Water controls Fire (extinguishes) -> Fire controls Metal (melts) -> Metal
controls Wood (axe cuts) -> back to Wood.

Being controlled is usually restrictive/pressuring (basis of Officer/Authority
stars 官殺). Controlling something else can be empowering but taxing (basis of
Wealth stars 財, since the Day Master "spends effort" to control the Wealth
element).

## Reading interactions between two charts or a chart and a year
When comparing two elements (e.g. this year's stem vs. the Day Master, or
person A's Day Master vs. person B's), first check: do they generate each
other, control each other, or are they the same element? That single check
determines whether the interaction reads as supportive, restrictive, or
matching/competitive before any Ten God naming is applied.
